# Acts registry (starting map)

## How to use this registry

This is the **starting point** for research, not the source of the answer. It was compiled by an AI model, with only a few items spot-checked against secondary sources, not act by act on the official text. Each act must be verified on the official source before being used in a conclusion (protocol in sources-and-verification.md).

The list is **not exhaustive**. Also look for acts not listed here: commencement regulations, statutory guidance, devolved equivalents (Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland), sector-specific legislation, and acts made after compilation.

**The "Confidence" column** only describes how sure the compiler was about the act's identifier and status, not whether the act is in force today:
- **H** (high): the identifier is very likely correct.
- **M** (medium): the identifier, number or status must be verified as a priority.
- **ID?**: the act exists or very likely exists, but its exact identifier (SI number, chapter) must be found on legislation.gov.uk.

**Extent column:** UK = whole UK; GB = England, Wales and Scotland; E = England only; NI = Northern Ireland; EU = applies in the EU (relevant through the EU nexus). Extent shown is the compiler's understanding: always check it on legislation.gov.uk.

Compilation date: October 2026. Items marked "(spot-checked Oct 2026)" were checked against secondary sources (law firm briefings, regulator pages) during compilation; they still need confirmation on the official text.

## Maintaining the registry

When a verification reveals a wrong identifier, a revoked act, a new commencement or a new act, tell the user what should be updated here. Do not assume the registry is up to date.
